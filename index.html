Bayes’ theorem, in plain language

Bayes answers one question: after seeing evidence, how should your belief in a hypothesis change? Start with a base rate (prior), weigh the evidence (likelihood), then normalize to get the posterior.

P(H | E) = P(E | H) · P(H)P(E)
H = hypothesis, E = evidence
Why people get this wrong
Humans often confuse P(E|H) with P(H|E). Bayes is the bridge between them.

What “evidence” means

In practice we compute P(E) by summing over mutually exclusive hypotheses:

P(E) = Σi P(E|Hi) · P(Hi)

You’ll see this explicitly in the Multi‑Hypothesis calculator.
